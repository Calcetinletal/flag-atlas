## Color lab, Flag Twins and network — 31 October 2026

The color lab uses all records with a nonempty, recognized detected palette as its denominator. A flag contributes to every detected color it contains; complete combinations group the entire detected set. The network ranks up to eight other flags with at least one shared color by the size of the intersection divided by the size of the union, breaking ties by country name. Neighbor-to-neighbor links require a score of at least two thirds. These links describe approximate color-set similarity and do not establish historical influence, common origin, shape similarity or equal color proportions. This revision does not independently verify the classifications or colors' historical meanings; the existing 1% pixel threshold can omit small details.

`test_palette.cjs` uses an independent bitmask implementation to check all 205 classified flags, all 42,025 ordered pair scores, every ranked neighborhood and displayed graph link, the nine color counts, all complete combinations, and interaction paths for the network, details and combination filter. Synthetic checks cover empty, duplicate, unsupported, disjoint and matching colors. `test_data.py`, `test_site.cjs` and `test_export.cjs` passed, and the default origin PNG was byte-identical to the prior export. The actual browser layout and interactions remain unverified because browser access was unavailable. Deployment identity must be checked again after publication.

## Animated color and year network — 31 October 2026

This replaces the prior fixed spanning forest. All 205 flags are movable circular nodes. The color score remains detected-color intersection over union; the year score ranks by absolute difference in the input's recorded origin year. Each flag proposes two closest links, with duplicate pairs merged and ties ordered by name. The force-directed layout uses all comparable pairs, plus collision and boundary forces; distances are approximate. For year layout, pairwise gaps are capped at 400 years and an additional left-to-right year force helps distinguish earlier and later flags. The seven rows with unknown origin years remain visible in a separate region and have no year-based links. Origin dates do not all mean the same type of event; no missing years are inferred.

`test_palette.cjs` independently checks both modes' complete link sets, all 42,025 color scores, ranked neighbors, unknown-year exclusions, node selection, zoom and synthetic pointer dragging. It verifies requestAnimationFrame changes node positions and runs a full 180-tick simulation for each mode, checking finite positions and that more similar pairs are closer on average. Optional canvas previews of both computed layouts were inspected; these are not browser screenshots. The exact supplied PathBIX result URL and a real browser were unavailable, so its appearance and actual browser pointer/touch behavior were not visually compared or tested. Color extraction and source dates retain their existing limitations.
