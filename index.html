    06 / THE SMALL PRINT

    There’s more to
    the story.

    Flags evolve. An origin can mean a design, its first adoption, or an approximate documented use—not necessarily the current version or the year a country became independent.

    The supplied dataset includes UN members, observer states and additional entities. Uncertain years remain unknown. Each card preserves its original notes and links to its sources; inclusion does not imply a position on political status.

    Color history and symbolism previews are attributed to a pinned archive of the CIA World Factbook. They are not necessarily official interpretations, and some refer to symbols beyond the colors. The archive may discuss a related variant; interpretations and designs change. Missing accounts are marked as unresolved.

    Flag illustrations are from FlagCDN and Wikimedia Commons and may differ from a dataset’s named variant. Color labels are approximate, calculated from image pixels; shades are grouped and details under 1% may be missed. Entries without an illustration are excluded from color filters.
